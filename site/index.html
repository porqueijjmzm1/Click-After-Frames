        How it works
        
A ghost jumps before you do

        
On every click the mod runs hidden copies of your icon through the level, tick by tick. If the click you just made hits something, it tries the same click a few ticks later and uses the smallest delay that lands safely.

      

      

        
      

      

        
1 · YOU CLICK
Too early
The mod catches the click before the game does. Nothing changes on screen yet.

        
2 · THE GHOST PREDICTS
Up to 130 ticks ahead
A hidden copy of your icon jumps now, then 1, 2, 3… ticks later, using orbs, pads and portals along the way.

        
3 · THE GAME GETS IT
On the right tick
If jumping now was already safe, the click goes straight through. If no delay saves you, it goes straight through too.

      

      

        4 ticks = 1 frame at 60 fps
        physics at 240 ticks/s
        stages from 0.5f to 8f, plus Custom
        prediction budget: 8 ms per click
      

    

  


  

    

      

        8 game modes
        
Every mode plays by its own rules

        
A cube jump is nothing like a wave zigzag. The buffer decides what a "safe" click is differently for each one.

      

      

    

  


  

    

      

        Features
        
More than holding a click

        
Prediction that understands orbs and triggers, memory per level, tools that show you what the mod is doing, and options for anyone who feels lag.

      

      

        

          

          
Path view

          
Press F10 and the prediction is drawn in the level: red is what the click would do now, green is what the game gets, a white dot is where a delayed click fires. In live mode, blue is doing nothing and yellow is clicking now.

          show-path · F10
        

        

          

          
Level memory and death heatmap

          
The mod remembers, per level and per spot, which delays worked and where you die. Delays that worked before are tried first, and the progress bar gets red marks where you fall the most.

          level-memory · death-heatmap · F11 forgets the level
        

        

          

          
Orb alignment

          
Pressed up to 4 frames before reaching an orb? The click waits until you're inside it, so it hits.

          orb-align · orb-timing
        

        

          
click 0.5f early

          
Training mode

          
The mod predicts everything but delays nothing. It only shows how early you clicked, so you learn the timing yourself.

          training-mode
        

        

          
SAVED

          
Flash and ding

          
The screen flashes green or a ding plays on every saved click, so you know when the buffer stepped in.

          save-flash · save-ding
        

        

          
Predicts move triggers

          
Spikes, blocks and orbs that a move or rotate trigger is about to move are predicted the way the game will move them, easing and duration included.

          predict-move-triggers
        

        

          
Auto-release and auto-click

          
In wave, robot and dash orbs the mod can let go for you when holding longer would crash. In practice it can even click when doing nothing would kill you.

          wave-auto-release · auto-click
        

        

          
Click Between Frames

          
With CBF, a click lands partway through a tick. The prediction starts from that exact moment.

          cbf-sync
        

        

          
Robust saves

          
Before a save is used, it's tested with you half a tick ahead and behind. Saves that only work at the exact pixel are swapped out.

          robust-saves
        

        

          
Low latency and performance

          
Feel the buffer as lag? Cap delays at 1 frame. FPS dropping when you click? Each click gets at most 4 ms.

          low-latency · performance-mode · auto-budget
        

        

          
Plans two clicks ahead

          
When a crash comes from something your next click would handle, after an orb or a portal, the ghost tries one more click.

          plan-next-click · predict-through-portals

        Questions
        
Before you install

      

      

        
Does the mod play for me?
No. It only moves clicks you already made: an early click is held until the tick where it works. If you don't click, nothing happens. The exceptions are auto-release and auto-click, which are off by default and only act in practice mode unless you allow them in normal runs.

        
Can I use it for leaderboard runs?
No. It's a practice tool. Outside practice mode the "CBF | stage" watermark shows while the buffer is on, and any TPS other than 240 changes the game's physics.

        
Does it work on phones?
Yes, on Android (32 and 64-bit). With no keyboard, open the menu with the mouse button on the pause menu; the console has its own button in the Tools tab. There's no iOS build yet.

        
Does it work with Click Between Frames?
Yes. With "Click Between Frames sync" on, the prediction starts from the exact point inside the tick where your click landed.

        
My FPS drops when I click.
Turn on Performance mode: each click gets at most 4 ms of prediction. "Auto time budget" already does this by itself while the game is dropping frames.

        
Clicks feel late.
Turn on Low latency (delays capped at 1 frame) or lower the stage with key 6. To learn the timing with no delay at all, use Training mode.

        
I had the old version installed.
v3.0 has a new ID (timbodev.input-buffer). Delete the old .geode file so both don't run together. Your settings, stage, per-mode frames and level memory are copied from the old ID the first time.
